A box center is an estimate of the visible lug-nut center. “Image centered” does not establish depth, socket angle, contact, or readiness to turn the nut.
How this becomes arm alignment
  1. Mount the camera rigidly near the socket and collect labeled footage at the expected working distances.
  2. Train the detector and inspect its results on new recordings.
  3. With the socket already aligned by hand, set its aim point at the intended working distance. An offset camera’s aim point changes with distance.
  4. Use the displayed image error to develop and calibrate small arm corrections. Depth, tool-axis alignment and contact need separate checks before robot control is connected.
